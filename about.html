The Project

What PlateShare is, who it serves, and why it matters.

Problem Statement

Food businesses regularly discard surplus food that is still safe to eat. At the same time, food banks and shelters struggle to keep up with demand. The gap isn't a lack of food. It's a lack of coordination: donors don't know who can take their surplus, recipients don't know what's available, and there's no simple way to schedule pickups or keep records.

Our Solution

PlateShare is a web and mobile platform that makes donating surplus food as easy as throwing it away.

Real-time donation listings

Donors post surplus food with category, quantity, and pickup window. Listings expire automatically when the window closes.

Location-based matching

Recipient organizations are notified about donations within their service area and can claim them right away.

Pickup coordination

Built-in scheduling and status updates keep both sides informed from claim to completed pickup.

Donation records & reporting

Donors receive a history of completed donations for their own records, and organizations can see their impact over time.

Who It Serves

Food donors

Restaurants, grocery stores, caterers, bakeries, and dining halls with surplus inventory.

Recipient organizations

Food banks, shelters, pantries, and community nonprofits serving people in need.

Communities

Less food in landfills and more meals reaching the people who need them.
