CS 410 · CS 411W · Old Dominion University

Getting surplus food to people who need it.

PlateShare is a centralized food donation platform designed to connect restaurants, grocery stores, and caterers with food banks, shelters, community organizations, and volunteer drivers. By simplifying donation listings, recipient matching, and pickup coordination, PlateShare aims to reduce unnecessary food waste and improve food accessibility within local communities.

About the Project View Deliverables

The Problem

Businesses throw away large amounts of edible food every day, while many households in the same communities experience food insecurity. Donating surplus food is often more difficult than discarding it because businesses lack an efficient way to find recipients, coordinate transportation, and track completed donations.

01 · FOOD WASTE

Unnecessary Food Disposal

Restaurants, grocery stores, and other food businesses frequently have surplus food that could be redistributed rather than discarded.

02 · FOOD INSECURITY

Limited Food Accessibility

Food banks, shelters, and charitable organizations need reliable access to food donations to support individuals and families experiencing food insecurity.

03 · LOGISTICS

Donation Coordination

Communication difficulties, limited transportation, and time-sensitive pickup requirements create barriers to successful food redistribution.

Additional research supporting the PlateShare problem statement can be found on our References page.

Customers and End Users

PlateShare is designed to support businesses, charitable organizations, and volunteers involved in the collection and redistribution of surplus food. Each group plays an important role in connecting available food with communities that need it.

CUSTOMERS

Participating Organizations

PlateShare's intended organizational customers include food businesses and charitable organizations seeking a more efficient way to manage food donations, coordinate pickups, and participate in local food redistribution.

END USERS

Food Donors

Restaurant employees, grocery store employees, caterers, and other authorized business representatives who use PlateShare to list available surplus food and manage donations.

END USERS

Recipient Organizations

Food bank employees, shelter staff, and nonprofit representatives who use PlateShare to discover available donations, claim food, and coordinate its receipt and distribution.

END USERS

Volunteer Drivers

Individuals who use PlateShare to receive delivery assignments, collect food donations from participating businesses, and transport them to recipient organizations.

END USERS

Platform Administrators

Authorized users responsible for managing accounts, monitoring platform activity, maintaining system integrity, and supporting participating organizations.

BENEFICIARIES

Individuals and Families

Community members experiencing food insecurity who benefit from increased access to donated food through participating food banks, shelters, and charitable organizations.

Our Solution

PlateShare proposes a centralized food donation platform that simplifies communication and coordination between food donors, recipient organizations, and volunteer drivers.

The platform will allow participating businesses to create donation listings containing information about available food, quantities, pickup locations, and pickup windows. Recipient organizations will be able to discover and claim suitable donations, while volunteers will assist with transportation when needed.

Through automated notifications, volunteer assignment, and donation tracking, PlateShare aims to reduce the logistical barriers associated with food redistribution and provide a more organized, accessible, and efficient donation process.

Reduce Food Waste

Provide businesses with a convenient way to redistribute surplus food before it is discarded.

Improve Food Accessibility

Connect recipient organizations with available donations that can support individuals experiencing food insecurity.

Simplify Coordination

Centralize donation listings, notifications, volunteer assignments, and delivery tracking within a single platform.

How PlateShare Works

PlateShare is designed to simplify the food donation process from the initial donation listing through successful delivery.

01 · POST

Donors List Surplus

Food businesses create donation listings containing the food type, quantity, pickup location, and available pickup window.

02 · NOTIFY

Recipients Receive Alerts

Relevant recipient organizations receive notifications about available donations that may meet their needs.

03 · CLAIM

Recipients Claim Donations

Recipient organizations review available donation listings and claim food that meets their needs and capacity.

04 · ASSIGN

Volunteers Are Assigned

When transportation is required, suitable volunteer drivers are identified based on relevant delivery requirements and availability.

05 · DELIVER

Food Is Transported

Volunteers collect the donated food from participating businesses and deliver it to the designated recipient organizations.

06 · TRACK

Donation Activity Is Recorded

Completed donations are recorded, allowing participating organizations to review donation history and monitor their redistribution efforts.

Project Goals

The primary objectives of PlateShare are to improve the efficiency of food redistribution and reduce barriers to successful donations.

Environmental Impact

Reduce the amount of edible surplus food that is unnecessarily discarded by participating businesses.

Community Impact

Support charitable organizations in providing food to individuals and families experiencing food insecurity.

Operational Efficiency

Simplify communication, donation management, and transportation coordination between participating users.

Accountability and Tracking

Maintain organized donation records and status updates to improve transparency throughout the redistribution process.
