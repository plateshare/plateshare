CS 410 · CS 411W · Old Dominion University

Getting surplus food to people who need it.

PlateShare connects restaurants, grocers, and caterers that have surplus food with food banks, shelters, and community organizations that can put it to use, before it goes to waste.

About the Project View Deliverables

The Problem

Businesses throw away large amounts of edible food every day, while many households in the same communities don't have enough to eat. Donating is often harder than discarding: there's no easy way to find a recipient, arrange pickup, or track what was given.

01 · POST

Donors list surplus

Food businesses post available surplus with quantity, type, and a pickup window in a few taps.

02 · MATCH

Recipients claim it

Nearby food banks and shelters are notified and can claim donations that fit their needs.

03 · TRACK

Impact is recorded

Every completed pickup is logged, giving donors records of what they gave and the impact it had.
